## Summary
In this notebook, we analyze a simulation performed at the Blue Brain Project and published in Romani et al. (2024). The simulation is part of a larger campaign designed to identify the parameter conditions that best induce theta oscillations in a rat CA1 model. Here, we focus on the simulation corresponding to the best-performing conditions identified in that campaign.
